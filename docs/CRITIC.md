# Critic brief (read this, nothing else)

You judge the Memmory v3 web app against the design spec v3 and the roadmap mockups. Reference crops: `docs/reference/mockups/{home,memories,story,shared,recap,capture}.png` (Profile has no v3 mockup: judge it against spec 5.6 below). Shots: `node tools/gauntlet.mjs <module> --quick` writes `docs/shots/<module>/<scene>-phone-x2-morning.png` and `summary.json`. Extra shots: `node tools/shot.mjs "/?showcase=<module>&scene=<scene>&persona=p5&now=2026-09-04T09:00" --out <name> [--scroll 900] [--tap "<css>"] [--vp phoneSmall|desktop] [--clock 2026-09-04T21:00:00]`.

Scale: 10 indistinguishable from the mockup and a shipped AAA app; 8.5 AAA with nits; 7 good; 5 programmer art; below 5 broken or off-spec. Pass = score >= 8.5 AND errors = 0 (console errors, page errors, failed requests, ready timeouts in summary.json). Never inflate; every issue points at a shot.

## Rule sheet (spec section 8, binding)

1. Two worlds. Gallery is dark: ground #101014, cards #1D1D25, inputs in cards #14141A, text pure #FFFFFF. Capture sheet is light: Dawn gradient #C8D4EC to #F5D6BC to #E0C5DC, white surfaces, slate text #1F2937. Profile is cream #FAF7F2. No other backgrounds.
2. No accent color. Functional only: error #DC6B5E; Spotify green #1DB954 only on song rows and the recap credit. Photography carries all other color.
3. Full saturation text always: never rgba, opacity or gray tiers on text. Hierarchy through size and weight only.
4. Font Geist 400/500/600/700. Four roles: display 30/600, headline 20/600 (22 in story headers, 17 for the question), body 16/400 (500 for row titles), label 12/600 (letter-spacing 0.4). Tab labels and photo badges may use 11; section 5 names a few meta sizes (12.5, 13.5, 11.5, 10.5, 15.5). Nothing else below 12. No italics, no uppercase eyebrows, no bold single words.
5. Dark separation from surface tone, never borders or shadows. Hairline rgba(255,255,255,0.14) only on the tab bar top edge. Text on photos always sits on a scrim.
6. Radii 12/18/24/32/pill: cards and tiles 18, story blocks and hero 20 to 24, sheets 32 top corners, buttons chips inputs badges pill.
7. Motion 200/600/900ms, one easing cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1), no bounce. A memory opening from its tile (900ms) is the signature animation. Text never fades out; content moves. Reduced motion = opacity only.
8. Navigation: bottom tab bar Home · raised plus (54px white circle, lifted 22px, shadow 0 8px 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)) · Memories; labels 11/600, active 4px dot. App launches into Home. Stories push with a 32px surface back button. Recap is a full screen modal. Detail screens have no tab bar.
9. Words: memory, story, recap, shared memory, moment, Home, Memories, Capture, Profile. Badges name who GENERATED a moment. Retired: Vault, Cortex, Cards, collective, Chat, Memorialize.
10. Copy: the app never talks about itself ("A question for you", never "Memmory asks"). Plain beats poetic ("Saved."). One idea per line. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no em dashes. Strings verbatim from the bank: Good morning. · A year ago today · A question for you · Elena added to your memory · A few more moments from Greece. · Latest · 12 memories · Nothing here yet. Start capturing a memory. · What you said · Jonas said · Playing then · Leo added the song · Related memories · 3 earlier nights with Marco · What do you remember? · Save · Saved. · Watch the story · Everyone · What do you want to keep? · Hold to talk · Camera · 2 moments · one memory · Save now · Saved · Saved as one memory · Not saved. Tap to retry. · Offline. Everything you send is kept and syncs later. · Private by default. Your memories are never posted anywhere.
11. Capture engine unchanged: camera tap = photo, hold = video; mic hold = voice note; transcription in the background; saved memories never leave the thread.
12. Photos always straight, full width of their block, rounded, never tilted. Icons 1.5 to 1.8px line, 24 grid, 44px targets. Emoji as UI forbidden.
13. When in doubt: quieter, darker ground, bigger photography, fewer words.
