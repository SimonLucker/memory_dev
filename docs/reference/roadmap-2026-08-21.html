Memmory · Visual product roadmap · 2026-08-21

The app no longer opens into a chat. It opens into your memories.

That is the whole redesign. You still capture in the same chat as today, and you open it with the plus button. Every other screen shows what you get back: memories, stories and recaps. Every mockup on this page is a real screen in the Expo MVP (app/).

Every exact value for your AI (colors, type, component specs, copy): design-foundation.md. Load it into your coding tool next to your task.

The loop

How the app works, in five steps.

1

Capture

Tap plus and send a photo, a voice note or a few words. Two taps from anywhere.

2

Add context

The app asks one question inside the memory. You answer by typing or speaking.

3

It understands

It reads what you said, where you were and who was there, and connects it to older memories.

4

It gives back

A story to scroll. A recap to watch. A shared memory that grows.

5

You return

Home shows what is new since last time, so there is always a reason to come back.

A memory is a story you scroll.

Screen

One scrolling page, built from blocks: a large photo on top, pairs of smaller photos, your voice note with what you said in text, the song, related memories, and one question at the end.

Purpose

A memory should read like a story, not like a list of files.

Interaction

Tap a photo to see it full screen. Tap play to hear the voice note. Answer the question by typing or speaking, then tap Save.

System

The app builds this page automatically (src/lib/story.ts): what you captured first comes first. When you answer the question, it is saved into the memory right away, and the confirmation is one word: Saved.

Feeling

Tell it a little more and the memory gets better. Never "please provide more data".

MVP status

Built. app/memory/[id].tsx renders live clusters and demo memories through the same composer.

Shared memories. Everyone adds to the same story.

Screen

The same story view plus three things: a row with the people, small badges on the photos showing who generated each moment, and a button for the recap. That is everything the shared view adds. No feeds, no threads.

Purpose

Seven people can add photos and voice notes, and it still reads as one story.

Interaction

Everyone is selected by default. The row scrolls; tap a person to see only their moments, tap Everyone to come back. Tap Watch the story for the recap.

System

The same page, filtered by person. It is always one story, never separate feeds.

Feeling

This is where our shared memories become one story.

MVP status

Built, seeded. One shared memory ships in the app, the same Greece story the pitch tells. Real multi-user sync comes after the MVP.

The recap. A short film of the memory.

Screen

A full screen player. Photos slowly zoom, your voice notes play over the pictures, and text you wrote appears as quotes on top of photos and videos. The song runs underneath. Captions only name the person.

Purpose

Prove that something beautiful comes back. The moment the product stops being an idea.

Interaction

It plays itself, like a story on Instagram. Tap right to move on, left to go back, close or finish to exit.

System

It advances by itself on a timeline, with progress bars at the top.

Feeling

It feels like my memory, not a slideshow. It ends without a slogan: the last thing you see is just the last moment.

Next

Simon is cutting a test recap to show the format, one that could work in the MVP. Later idea: tag your friends in the recap, and it is sent straight to them.

MVP status

Built, composed by hand. app/recap/[id].tsx plays hand-built sequences for Greece and Marco's. The app composing recaps by itself, and real film rendering, come after the raise.

The words

Keep the language simple and let what comes back carry the feeling. The less the app talks about itself, the more intelligent it feels.

It never talks about itself

No "Memmory asks", "Memmory wove", "Memmory kept". Write what the user sees: "A question for you", "Related memories", "Saved".

Plain beats poetic

The richer memory is visible on screen. It does not need announcing. When in doubt, write the shortest true sentence.

People's words carry the feeling

"Best pasta of my life. Marco finally met Sofia." is the strongest line in the product. Nothing we write should compete with it.

One idea per line

Never a headline plus an explanation plus an emotional conclusion in the same place. Strong screens are allowed to just end.

Three words, used precisely

A memory is the thing. A story is how a memory reads. A recap is the film. No other synonyms, anywhere in the UI.

Five flows to test

Each one works from start to finish in the MVP. Test them in this order.

1

Capture a memory

Tap plus, send a photo and a voice note, tap Save now. Open Memories: the moment is already a story with a big photo, the text of what you said, and one question at the end.

2

Explore a memory

Open Dinner at Marco's. Scroll the story, tap a photo to see it full screen, play the voice note, see the song and the related memories.

3

Open a shared memory

Open The house in Greece. Seven people, and each moment is marked with who generated it. Tap Elena and see the same week through only her moments.

4

Watch a recap

From Home or from the memory itself. Photos slowly zoom, Jonas's voice note plays, the song runs, and it ends on the last moment.

5

Come back tomorrow

Open the app the next day. Home shows what is new: a finished story, a year ago today, a question, Elena's new photos. Never an empty input field.

North star

This is not a place where you store photos. This is a place where your memories become something.
